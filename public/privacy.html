Location data

We use your location only while you are clocked in, and only for the reasons below. Tracking stops the moment you clock out.

Everyone, at clock-in. When you clock in, the app checks you are within 75 metres of your workplace. We record that one position with your timesheet. We do not track staff who are not delivery drivers.

Delivery drivers, during a shift. If your role involves deliveries, the app records your position in the background while you are clocked in, including when the app is closed or your screen is locked. We use this to:

  • measure the one-way distance of each delivery run, from the store to your last drop, so extra mileage is paid correctly
  • show managers where drivers are during a shift, so deliveries can be coordinated

The return journey to the store is not recorded as paid mileage. When you tap Delivered, we record the position of that drop.

When it stops. Background tracking starts when you clock in and stops when you clock out, or when your shift is closed automatically. It never runs while you are off shift.

How you'll know. Before tracking starts, the app explains what is recorded and asks your permission. On iPhone a blue location indicator shows at the top of the screen while it is running. On Android a notification stays visible while it is running.

Your control. You can turn off location permission at any time in your device settings. If you do, you can still enter your mileage by hand and a manager can check it.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit and access is restricted at the database itself, not only in the app.

  • Each business's data is kept separate from every other business's.
  • Staff see only their own records. Managers see only staff at the locations they manage. Pay is visible only to administrators.
  • Every connection to the app uses encryption.
  • These access rules are checked by automated tests before every change is released.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will tell your employer without undue delay so they can inform you and the Information Commissioner's Office where required.

Your rights

You have the right to see the information held about you, correct it, and in some cases have it deleted or restricted.

  • Access — ask for a copy of your information, including manager notes written about you.
  • Correction — ask for anything inaccurate to be fixed. A manager can correct timesheets and mileage.
  • Deletion and restriction — ask for information to be deleted or its use limited, where the law allows.
  • Objection — object to uses based on legitimate interests, including driver location tracking.
  • Portability — receive your information in a usable format.

Most requests should go to your employer, as the controller. You can also contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will pass it on. We respond within one month.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself, in the app under Profile settings. When you do:

  • your login is removed permanently and you can no longer sign in
  • your name, email and contact details are removed
  • your location history, pending requests and notifications are deleted
  • your worked hours and pay records are kept, with your name removed, because employers are legally required to retain payroll records

This cannot be undone.
